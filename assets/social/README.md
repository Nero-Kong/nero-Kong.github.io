and marks belong to their respective owners. The email link uses the Gmail
icon as requested; its original university mailto address is unchanged.

- `github.svg`: https://github.githubassets.com/assets/pinned-octocat-093da3e6fa40.svg

homepages. The X asset was linked from its official brand toolkit:
https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit

The Gmail asset was linked from https://workspace.google.com/products/gmail/.
